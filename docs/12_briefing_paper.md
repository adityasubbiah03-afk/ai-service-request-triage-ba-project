## Purpose
To seek approval to fund Phases 0 and 1 (approvals and shadow-mode testing) of an AI-assisted triage capability, and to record the conditions attached.

## Recommendation
That the EGM:
1. **Approve** $220,000 (indicative) for Phase 0 (approvals) and Phase 1 (shadow mode); release further funding only if Gate 2 criteria are met.
2. **Nominate** an accountable use case owner before Phase 1.
3. **Endorse** staff and staff-representative consultation starting now.
4. **Note** that the tool will suggest only and will never decide or contact citizens.

## Key points
1. **The problem.** Every request is triaged by hand: an assumed 25,000 officer hours a year, with peak backlogs, re-routing and a risk that urgent hardship cases wait in routine queues.
2. **The proposal.** A tool that suggests which team should handle a request, flags urgent cases, shows its reasons, and sends uncertain cases to people. Safety and hardship rules run first and cannot be overridden by the model. Every output is reviewed by an officer during the pilot.
3. **The evidence so far.** On synthetic data (no real citizen data used), the prototype suggested a route for 86.6% of requests, was right 95.0% of the time when it did, sent 4.5% to a manual queue and escalated 8.9% as priority. These results show feasibility only and are likely to be higher than real-world performance.
4. **A concern we found.** Accuracy differed by writing style by up to 8.2 percentage points, above our 5-point limit. This must be understood and treated before any pilot, and re-tested on real data.
5. **The money.** In the base case, three-year net benefit is about $0.9 million, similar to a rules-only alternative ($0.88 million). In a pessimistic case Option 2 loses about $0.3 million. Benefits are released staff capacity, not cash savings. All figures rest on assumptions to be replaced with agency data.
6. **Governance.** A Privacy Impact Assessment, security authorisation and an AI use case assessment under the Digital Transformation Agency's AI policy are required before real data is used. Indicative risk rating is Medium, treated as elevated for the hardship pathway.

## Risks (top three)
| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Synthetic results overstate real-world performance | Shadow mode on real data before any staff exposure |
| Unequal performance across groups | 5-point limit, treatment before pilot, advocacy consultation |
| Staff over-trust suggestions | Show confidence and reasons, training, blind audits, override monitoring |
